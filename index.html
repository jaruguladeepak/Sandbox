The EcoBin Pipeline

An end-to-end automated lifecycle that completely eliminates reactive waste collection.

📡
1. Sense
ESP32 & Ultrasonic sensors map bin fill-levels in real-time.
🧠
2. Predict
XGBoost ML forecasts exactly when bins will reach capacity.
🗺️
3. Optimize
OR-Tools dynamically routes trucks to priority zones only.
🚛
4. Collect
Efficient, fuel-saving collection with zero overflows.
